Conics not determined by their input

Some conic constructions have no definite result for special input: a conic tangent to the same line twice, or through the same point twice. Their formulas then compute rounding noise, a different arbitrary conic in every step. Tracing used to follow that noise with ever smaller steps, up to its limit of 10000 refinements, in every move: pages like this one took about a minute to load, and dragging took seconds per move. Now such a conic is undefined (not drawn), the other one stays, and dragging is as fast as usual.

Tangent to the same line twice

A conic with pole A and polar a (red), through B, tangent to the purple line c, and to c again. One of the two conics is still determined, the other one is not and is undefined. Drag any point or line: the page should respond immediately.

Two tangent lines brought together

The same construction, tangent to the purple lines c (through C1, C2) and d (through D1, D2). Drag D1 onto the grid point (-4, -3), or : c and d coincide, and one conic becomes undefined. Drag D1 away again, or : both conics are back, and they keep their colors (tracing continues from before the coincidence). The move onto the coincidence is reported as BAD, as any move into a singularity.

Through the same point twice

Conics through A, B, C, D, tangent to the blue line l. Drag D onto C, or : through three points and tangent to a line leaves a whole family of conics, so neither of the two is determined, and nothing is drawn (the move onto C is reported as BAD, as any move into a singularity). Drag D away again, or : both conics are back.
